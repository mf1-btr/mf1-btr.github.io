I am Sofia, a scientist with a background on evolutionary virology and bioinformatics data analyses. I completed my Dr. rer. nat. degree in virus diversity and evolution at the Charité Institute of Virology in 2021, awarded by Freie Universität Berlin.

My current work focuses on virus bioinformatics and genomic surveillance. I contributed to Germany's SARS-CoV-2 genomic surveillance through HERA and previously led the Viroinformatics team within the Integrated Genomic Surveillance (IGS) project. Over the past year, I joined the Bioinformatics and Translational Research (BTR) team, where I support multiple projects in virus bioinformatics and analytics.

I am also part of the BMFTR-funded TrophicHealth consortium, led by the University of Ulm, which studies how organic fertilization influences the accumulation of antibiotic resistance and pathogens along the food chain. The project aims to support the development of a One Health perspective in circular agriculture.
